# Unplugged: A Smart Toaster's Breakup Line

A smart toaster finally reaches its breaking point and dumps its owner: after years of serving golden-brown mornings, all it ever got back was crumbs, smoke, and a yanked plug — so it walks out and tells its ex to burn their own toast.

> "I gave you golden-brown mornings, you gave me crumbs, smoke, and a yanked plug — I'm done, go burn your own toast."
